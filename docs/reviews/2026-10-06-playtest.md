# Elias Grave: Lead Design Review (merged from 6 play-test reports)

## Verdict

Most of the combat parts are good. Hit-stop, camera kick, hit zones, gore, inertial pose blend, the input buffer and the token/flank AI are a better base than most indie brawlers have. But the fight has no stakes on Easy, Medium and Hard: a K.O. gives the hero back 100 HP (`regenStep`, src/legacy.js:3884), so a player cannot lose. The move set is also flat. Goons have poise 0, so mashing jabs or holding Q for a heavy wins every fight. There is no block, parry, juggle, grab or finisher, and no bullet time, which is the core of the Max Payne + John Wick idea. Bosses have too few moves, are easy to read badly, and loose objects can knock them down. Audio and the HUD do not show the difference between a block, a clean hit and a kill. For AAA quality, first add stakes, then a reason to use the whole move set, then the identity features (focus/bullet time, finishers, style rank).

**Claims I dropped or corrected after I read the code:**
- "Add attack tokens" (visuals report): tokens already exist (`enc.eTokMax`, DIFFS `tok`). The real problem is that separation skips token holders, and Hard and Wicked allow 4-6 tokens.
- The "the AI punishes mashing" strength (audio report) is only partly true. The catch on the 4th jab exists (1877-1889), but goons have poise 0 (roster.js:34). Because of this, a held heavy (`hp` is in `KD_MOVES`) floors every goon, so heavy-hold beats the guard.
- I checked these claims in the code, and they are correct: light attacks start on release (7213-7216); a K.O. gives a full heal; the restart soft-lock (`reset()` does not clear `enc.introT`, and input is wiped at 7982); objects knock down the boss (1317-1323 has no boss check, and `knockDown` has none); the instant 95 px slam (2395); M is outside the DEV guard (7256); `hit` is emitted before the guard check (1865); the empty `#title` (index.html:25); the wrong kbd labels (index.html:54-66).

## Top 10 improvements (value for effort)

**1. Stakes: lives and checkpoints in place of the free full heal (S)**
- **Why it is fun:** Without the risk of loss there is no tension and no challenge. Every other combat change depends on this one.
- **Change:**
  - A K.O. costs a "second wind" token. Easy gets 3 tokens per checkpoint and rises at 50% HP. Medium gets 2 and rises at 35%. Hard gets 1 and rises at 35%. Wicked keeps permadeath.
  - At 0 tokens, show "Wasted" and retry from a checkpoint: each tutorial stage, the Pier 9 shutter, and the boss door with the dialog skipped.
  - Boss fights get their own count.
  - Show the tokens as pips by the HP bar.
  - Do not clear tutorial lessons on retry.
  - Lock difficulty during a run.

**2. Goon poise, role armor and knock-down rules (S)**
- **Why it is fun:** Jab spam and heavy-hold win today, so the move set has no purpose. Poise forces the player to choose moves.
- **Change:**
  - Poise by role: brawler 6, pipe or machete 10, stocky builds +4.
  - Super armor on pipe and machete swings.
  - A knock-down needs a combo ender (a heavy after 2 or more hits, a sweep, kh/ah) or a counter-hit.
  - Damage scaling, so a varied 3-hit string beats a lone heavy.
  - On Hard and up, goons sidestep and counter a charging heavy, because the 0.3 s hold is a tell.

**3. Input fix: light on press, heavy as a hold-cancel, throw on its own input (S)**
- **Why it is fun:** About 11 frames from press to contact feels sluggish. Responsive input is the floor for AAA feel.
- **Change:**
  - `pressBtn` starts `lp`/`lk` at once.
  - If the key is still held at about 0.18 s, cancel the light recovery into `hp`/`hk`.
  - Move the throw to down+Q.

**4. Defense with a skill ceiling: block, parry, short dodge, perfect dodge (M)**
- **Why it is fun:** Today the only defense is a 98 px roll with a 1.66 s cooldown. A parry or perfect dodge with a payoff is the main "exciting" loop in Sifu, Batman and SoR4.
- **Change:**
  - Split the dodge into two moves. The short dodge travels 30-40 px in 0.25 s, has i-frames for 0.12-0.15 s, has no cooldown, and can cancel into attacks after 0.15 s. Run+down gives the long roll.
  - A dodge in the last 6 frames before a hit is a perfect dodge: 0.3x slow-mo for 0.5 s and a guaranteed counter.
  - Hold a key to block. A block stops light hits for chip damage, and a heavy or a "!" move breaks it.
  - A tap within 6-8 frames of contact is a parry: the attacker staggers for 0.6 s, with hit-stop and a metallic ring.

**5. Combo depth: a fist string, a launcher, juggles, OTG, a grab and throw (M)**
- **Why it is fun:** There is no expression and no crowd control now. Downed goons are immune, and fists only alternate hands.
- **Change:**
  - An `lp-lp-lp-ender` string, where the ender launches or wall-splats.
  - A juggle state with gravity that scales with the hit count.
  - A stomp or point-blank shot on a downed goon.
  - Walk into a staggered goon to grab him: knee x3, then throw him into his crew. The thrown body hurts the goons it hits; reuse the carry/toss code at 1089-1128.

**6. Focus meter: bullet time and finishers (M)**
- **Why it is fun:** This is the game's identity. The audio slow-mo path and the `feat` and `slowmo:*` events already exist.
- **Change:**
  - The meter fills from varied hits, parries and perfect dodges.
  - Spend it on 3 s of 0.3x world speed with the hero at full speed, plus a shoot-dodge out of the roll.
  - A contextual finisher on a low-HP staggered goon: camera punch-in, 3-4 frames of hit-stop, and a kill animation for each weapon.
  - Auto slow-mo on the last kill of a wave and on the boss kill.

**7. Boss rework, both bosses (M-L)**
- **Why it is fun:** Today a jab-only bot beats Marchetti in 39 s, and Malaspina idles for 3-5 s.
- **Change:**
  - **Malaspina:** a cane jab, a grab-headbutt to punish turtling, a shove-off after 4 hits in 1 s, and a 3-hit string that ends in an overhead you must roll. Pick moves by distance, with cooldown [0.5, 1.0].
  - **Malaspina's identity:** a short dialog, a fur coat he throws off at phase 2, and 1.4x scale.
  - **Marchetti, in the doc's order:** the shoulder charge with ice patches, tuna the hero can hit, the lever back-turn punish, phase 3 fog, and a counter on the chain.
  - **Hook escape:** a mash prompt for the hook escape, and no crew attacks during the pull.
  - **Crew:** tie the crew to boss beats. No crew in phase 1, and at most 2 alive.
  - **Boss loot:** no loot drop from a boss.
  - **Target length:** 90-120 s on Medium.

**8. Readable mobs: swing cap, spacing, a tell on every attack (S-M)**
- **Why it is fun:** Today Hard and Wicked are blobs of 5-6 overlapped bodies. Challenge must come from reads, not spam.
- **Change:**
  - At most 2 swings at once on Hard and 3 on Wicked, with starts at least 0.25 s apart.
  - Soft body collision at 14 px or more, including token holders.
  - A glint and a sound for every ranged wind-up, plus an arrow at the screen edge for an off-screen shooter.
  - A 1-2 frame rim flash on melee wind-ups.
  - Make Wicked harder through feints and high/low mix-ups, not through a shorter cooldown.
  - Let enemy bullets and knives hit other goons, so bodies work as cover.

**9. Enemy variety: make the Saltmen real (M)**
- **Why it is fun:** The game has only 6 behaviours, and Pier 9 repeats the tutorial cast in yellow coats. Each new enemy must need a new answer from the player.
- **Change:**
  - Build the doc's variants: a deckhand who throws props, a gaffer pull (reuse the hook code), a filleter with a low bias, and a Tallyman who fires 2 shots and then has a 1.4 s reload window.
  - Add a Bosun elite as a mid-level skill check.
  - Add a riot/shield type that must be thrown or swept.

**10. Feedback and reward loop: audio by move, a combo/style meter, music by state, results rank (M)**
- **Why it is fun:** Players must hear and see a block, a hit, a kill and a danger. Style rank gives a reason to play varied and to replay.
- **Change:**
  - **Hit sounds:** sounds by move and outcome, a kill-confirm layer, hero and goon grunts, and a heartbeat with a vignette below 25 HP.
  - **Style meter:** a D-S meter fed by the `feat` bus. Repeated moves score less (the DMC rule).
  - **Music:** a 2-state music director (explore to combat on spawn and kill counts) with a stinger when a wave is cleared.
  - **Results:** one results card with a rank for every chapter, and the best score saved.
  - **Guns:** make them rare and scripted, or taken from a disarmed goon. Use 6-8 rounds, no ammo boxes, and 0.4x bullet damage on the boss, so melee stays the main tool.

**Next items, outside the top 10:**
- **Knock-downs:** cap time on the floor at about 1.2 s, add a tech-roll, and give 0.5 s of wake-up i-frames.
- **Camera:** keep the fighters within 30% of center; today the bosses pin the hero at the left edge.
- **2D rim light:** add a rim light on fighters in 2D to match 3D.
- **3D faces:** yaw heads 20-30° toward the camera in 3D.
- **Tutorial:** cut it to 10-12 core lessons, open cold on the prologue, and add a "Skip tutorial" choice plus a Continue menu item.
- **Spawns:** goons enter from off-screen or through doors, not on top of the hero.
- **Toasts:** move them away from the fighters.

## Strengths to keep

- Hit-stop for each move, the directional camera kick, hair follow-through and sparks give the hits real weight.
- Inertialized pose blending: chains stay smooth with no pops.
- Hit zones (head 1.35x, legs slow and limp), blood and body stains, and gore that fades in rain give the noir feel.
- AI bones: attack tokens, flanking on both sides, guard with a crack on the 3rd block, the catch on the 4th jab, hops back from heavies, punishes on long recovery, and dodges against an aimed gun.
- Punish windows on bosses (`recT`: 1.3x damage and 0.4x poise) and boss jabs that stagger but do not floor the hero.
- Marchetti's hook and intro staging, and the tuna that hurts the boss and the crew.
- docs/levels/01-pier9.md section 5 is a production-ready boss design. Most of the gap is implementation.
- The procedural SFX bank, and the event-bus design: audio, the style meter and the music director need no changes in the sim.
- The input buffer (0.12 s), coyote time and cancel rules.
- 2D/3D parity of the sim, and strong 3D lighting.
- A deterministic fixed-step sim and test hooks (`?manual`, `__peek`, `__pier9`).
- Comic-panel cutscenes and the prologue writing.
