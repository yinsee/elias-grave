## 3. Unfair, but not blocking

- **Ch7: an Usher shoots from another car** that Elias cannot reach (7 HP a hit, about 1 per second; Medium bot K.O. twice, Hard wasted). Ranged goons must not fire when they are not in the hero's car group (train/run.js:401).
- **Ch8: the FINISH on Bell** fails with W, with a blade, or with a heavy started too close (its window: 71 frames on Medium, 47 on Wicked). The toast says "hold Q or W". Accept any hit during the window, or any blow with power ≥ 200 (crown.js:153, bell.js:35).
- **Hard: a fall heals.** A fall retries from the checkpoint at 100 HP (HP 20 → fall → 100), while Easy and Medium lose 30 HP. Hard is kinder than Medium here (legacy.js:5132, train/run.js:242).
- **Ch2: refuge niches are not safe.** A Lineman walked into the hero's niche and hit him twice during a train; any attack steps him out of the niche (metro/run.js:218).
- **Goons out of reach:** Ch8 lift rappellers land on the car roof with no ledge rule (a Forker threw 46 forks in 60 s); a Ch6 spotter stood on a planter for 4 minutes so the wave never ended; Line Zero perched Rats do not come down; a tutorial pistol goon shoots from behind the next stage gate.
- **Falls that reset a whole area:** Kessel's first hook pulls Elias into the gap (Hard: restart the garden; Wicked: the level); Lenz on Hard has no phase-3 checkpoint, so one slip restarts him at full HP.
- **Free boss damage:** Krieg takes damage before his fight starts (17 % with the ER machine gun on Hard), and a retry gives the gun back.
- **Kalt:** a death in phase 2 or 3 restarts her at phase 1 with full HP.

## 4. Difficulty: what is uneven, and suggestions

**What the numbers show**
- Damage per hit does not change with difficulty. Difficulty only changes enemy counts, boss stats and regen.
- Boss HP (Medium) drops after Chapter 1: Marchetti 1050, then Switch 820, Brask 840, Kalt 840.
- Too easy: Marchetti (31 HP lost on Hard), Tinder (7-15 s), the Porter (0 HP lost on Hard), Lys (15-30 s, even on Wicked), Brask's hall on Easy (no damage).
- Too hard for its place: Line Zero trains, Teague and Kalt (Ch4 is a big step up from Ch3), Vane on Medium (8 tries; about 30 % of the damage comes from sniper dots while she is idle), Bell phase 1 on Easy and Medium.
- Checkpoints refill second winds but not HP, so the bot reached bosses with 3-40 HP (Grau 5, Kessel 3, Pier 9 boss 19). Dying gives more HP than arriving.
- Rules differ by level: crews with no total cap (Brask, Cooler, Teague), Vane allows 5 crew alive on Wicked (others 3), fire does 5-8 HP/s in the casino and 10-22 in Line Zero, the train `b2` wave reinforces on Easy and Medium.
- Wicked: nothing past the tutorial street was cleared by the bot. Wicked is meant to be very hard, but the falls that restart a level and the off-screen shooters make it feel unfair, not hard.

**Suggestions (a shared difficulty model first, then per boss)**
1. Scale damage taken by difficulty: ×0.7 Easy, ×1.0 Medium, ×1.15 Hard, ×1.3 Wicked.
2. Checkpoint HP: on first arrival at a boss or section checkpoint, set HP to max(HP, 60) on Easy and Medium, max(HP, 40) on Hard. A retry never gives more HP than arriving.
3. One fall rule for every level: Easy/Medium −30 HP and back on the ledge; Hard −40 HP and one second wind; Wicked lethal. Show "From the start" on Wicked, with the Wasted card.
4. A smooth boss HP curve (Medium): Malaspina 800, Marchetti 900, Switch 900, Brask 920, Kalt 940, Krieg 960, Vane 1000, Lenz 1080, Bell 1200; Hard ×1.2, Wicked ×1.4.
5. One crew rule: a total cap per boss crew ([6, 8, 10, 12]) and at most 3 alive.
6. A phase checkpoint in phase 3 of every boss on Easy, Medium and Hard.
7. Per boss: raise Marchetti (+20 % HP or a phase-1 crew), Tinder (HP ×2), Porter (poise 20 → 40, HP +25 %), Lys (HP 360-630, decoys from Medium). Lower Teague (faceplate 80 → 50 HP), Kalt (keep her phase on a retry; dive tell 0.6 → 0.9 s on Easy/Medium), Vane on Medium (1 dot per "Mark!", sniper hit 30 → 22), Bell phase 1 on Easy/Medium (HP or crew cap), Line Zero trains (30/40/55/70).
8. Put a bandage before Marchetti and before Malaspina (Hard and Wicked have no regen).

## 5. Graphics

1. **The hero is hard to find in a crowd** (casino, Spire): hero and goons wear black suits and dark hair. Give the hero one fixed mark no goon shares (a warm 1 px rim light, or a visible white shirt and red tie), or tint each faction's suits.
2. **The camera is too tight in fights** (160-270 world px): attackers and shooters are often off screen. Keep at least ~340-360 px wide while 2+ goons are near, and widen until every attacker with a token is in frame.
3. **2D/3D mismatches:** the Palm House has no 3D models for its moving props (statues used as cover, guests, towers, chandeliers, lantern); the casino lobby shows a brick wall in 3D instead of the mirrors; Bell's 2D head differs from his 3D model.
4. **Leaks:** the Club Noir blade sign and chevrons show in the Line Zero yard and the aquarium (`drawStairSign` guard, legacy.js:6133: draw it only on the street).
5. **Light:** dark interiors are darker in 3D (raise the minimum ambient by ~0.08); bloom washes out the theatre lamp and ballroom windows (−40 %), so Krieg's wind-up glint is hard to read; white suits (Brask, Lenz) burn out in 3D.

## 6. UI and onboarding

1. **The tutorial is long and teaches the wrong things:** 66 lessons gate the street (25 in "Bare hands"); the lesson list covers ~22 % of the screen. It never teaches niches, train warnings, levers, hitting props, or shooters up high, which are what cost HP in chapters 1-2. Collapse the list by default, make the grab sub-lessons optional, and add a short hazard lesson (one harmless train, a niche, a lever).
2. **Hidden verbs need a prompt:** the casino vault hatch, the orchid glass cabinets, "↑ climb out" when drowning, the Spire snap ("hold ↑ and →"), the Ch8 hatch objective arrow (↓ should be ↑).
3. **The title screen hides the "Locked" toast** (css/game.css:223), so a new player does not know why chapters are locked.
4. **Small fixes:** objective text after a retry (Palm House), the "fi" ligature shows "The fishbowl" as "The Ashbowl" (`font-variant-ligatures: none`), style-rank badge over the objective, PA lines in the middle of the fight lane, final card says "2 of 9" for 8 chapters, volume wraps 0 → 100 %, ◄ ► on locked values, a tiny SKIP button and no text-speed option, "$690" reads as "S690", 640×360 layout overlaps, a run started with a test hook saves "New best".
5. **No touch or gamepad controls.** Add touch buttons, or a "keyboard needed" note on phones.

## 7. Performance

One frame costs 4-19 ms on average (M1 Max, other browsers open), with the slowest 5 % up to ~50 ms. The CPU 2D passes cost most: `postPass` 22 %, `lightPass` 14 %, Pier 9 `drawSky` 13 %. A level's first frames spike to 0.9-1.8 s. Cache Pier 9's sky and backdrop, run the light and post passes at half resolution, and warm up 3D shaders during the chapter intro.

## 8. Suggested order of work

1. **Blockers B1-B6** and the FINISH input, the off-screen Usher and the Hard fall heal. Small, local fixes; each needs a browser check.
2. **The shared difficulty model** (section 4, points 1-6). One change in shared code, then a bot run of every boss on all four difficulties.
3. **Per-boss tuning** (section 4, point 7), checked with the boss bots.
4. **Readability:** camera width, hero mark, Club Noir sign leak, 3D gaps.
5. **Onboarding and UI:** tutorial trim and hazard lesson, prompts for hidden verbs, small UI fixes.
6. **Performance.**
