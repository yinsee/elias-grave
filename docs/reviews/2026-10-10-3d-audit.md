# 3D audit: the 3D view against the 2D view, every level (2026-10-10)

Branch `worktree-agent-a4f2f9e9fe5dc2aa1` (from main 14a216e). Method: for each level and section, the same spot in both views
(`index.html?manual`, the level hooks `window.__<id>.start() / skip() / toBoss()`, `tp(x)`, `view3d(false/true)`), 1280x720.
Screenshots: the session scratchpad, folder `3d-audit/` (`<tag>_<level>_<x>_pair.png`: 2D on top, 3D below; `_2d.png` and
`_3d.png` full size). Tags: `a_` = before (main), `b_`/`c_` = steps in between, `d_` to `g_` = the per-fix checks, `h_` = after
all fixes. Only 3D models and 3D-only drawing changed, plus shared painters split into one function per mover (2D draws the same
pixels as before) and one shared 2D fix (the street's signs and rails on the street only).

## Real glass, mirrors, water and haze (no dither; G3 replaced)

The user's decision: no dotted (screen-door) effects in 3D. G3's 4x4 ordered dither is gone (`T3_BAYER`, the `dith` path in
`layer()`), and so is Pier 9's dithered freezer fog. In their place, real three.js surfaces. Only 3D drawing changed: the level
painters leave their glass pixels out when `R.glass3d` is set (3D layers only) and their level `three.js` names the glass instead.

| Part | How (src/legacy.js Three3D unless named) |
|---|---|
| See-through pixels left on a level's layers (sprays, bubbles, jelly glow, sniper lines, Pier 9's fog) | The level layers are transparent (`alphaTest` 0.01) and blend each pixel by its alpha; such pixels are lit like the rest. `blendPlot` over an already see-through pixel composites "over" (it made them opaque) and keeps alphas off the 250-254 flags. The street's layers stay cut-outs. |
| Glass: tank fronts, cases, the jelly cylinder, ward and fishbowl windows, cabinets, the lift car, train windows, roof panes | `{ pane }` from a level's `models()`: a thin box at its own depth (on the wall, a case's box, or a slab across the lane for the panes that wall it off; roof panes lie flat). One shader (`paneMat`): faint tint, Fresnel, the scene mirrored in it (the existing wall mirror image `rtWin`, or `rtFlr` for a surface facing up), a Blinn glint per lamp, lit edges, a sliding sheen. Cracks: the level's own crack painter drawn once per crack state into a texture on the pane (`crack.draw`), or rays in depth for a pane across the lane. A hit flash lights the pane. |
| Mirrors (House Always Wins: the lobby and hall mirrors, pillars, swivels, the end wall) | `kind: 'mirror'`: silver lit by the room plus a clear `rtWin` reflection, so the fighters show in them. A glass decoy has no reflection (the Count's tell): `updateFighters` rigs no-reflection bodies last and `reflect()` leaves them out (also Lys, the Spire). The ripple tell runs down the pane (`rip`). |
| Water (the Leviathan's floods, the pump hall, the sea channel, the tunnel) | `kind: 'water'`: a tinted volume from the wall to the street's edge; its skin moves (two crossing waves bend the normal), light ribbons play in it, a lit chop on top; whoever is in it shows through. |
| The Spire's glass floor | The city box under a glass tile (`kind: 'floor'`) that mirrors whoever stands over it; the stage cracks on it. |
| Mist, steam, smoke | `{ mist }` (the orchid rooms: four soft banks of haze at four depths, folding slowly) and `R.soft()` puffs (one `Points` draw, round soft-edged dabs): Palm House pipe steam, the dining car's steam jet, Krieg's canister, flare smoke, the dealer's cigarette, the Spire's soup, pipe and clutch smoke, the lift's clouds. |
