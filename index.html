    A tutorial level, left to right. The panel at the top of the game view shows one lesson at a time and ticks it off when you do it; L shows the stage's whole list. Walk into a weapon to pick it up. You carry one weapon. With empty hands you walk over a weapon to take it; with a weapon in hand you squat (↓) on another to swap, and the old one drops. Hold Q with no goon in reach to throw your weapon; walk over it to take it back. Guns do not refill by themselves: enemies sometimes drop ammo boxes, and you walk over a box to load it. A fire escape zig-zags up the club building to its roof. Space is a short dodge (in the air, a dash); ↓ at a run is a long roll. Attacks cannot hit you at the start of a dodge. Hold E to block; tap E just before a hit lands to parry. Drag a body part to throw the fighter, or click near the body or an object to hit it. Goons wait in each stage and hit back; the boss waits at the far end. R restarts the tutorial. Demo (M) plays the whole tutorial by itself; press any control to take over. F switches to a 12 fps sprite look.
